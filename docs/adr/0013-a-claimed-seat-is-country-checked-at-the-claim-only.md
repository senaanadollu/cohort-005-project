# A Claimed Seat is country-checked at the claim, not on playback

A Student who bought a course at a Purchasing-Power Price can only play it in the country they bought it from (ADR 0009). That check reads the Student's own Individual Purchase: the one that still stands, meaning it has no Approved Refund Request. A Student enrolled through a Claimed Seat has no Purchase of their own, and we decided not to follow the Seat back to its Team Licence: the claim already refuses a Student in a different country from the Team Licence, and blocking a Student who claimed in-country and later travels would be a new restriction nobody asked for.

## Considered Options

- **Check playback against the Team Licence behind the Claimed Seat.** Rejected for now: it changes who is restricted, where this decision was only about which Purchase the check reads.

## Consequences

A Student enrolled through a Claimed Seat on a discounted Team Licence can play the course from any country once they have claimed. Closing that is a policy change, not a bug fix, and needs its own decision.
